The problem

Paper, denials, and expired credentials cost fleets money

Every one of these is a claim denied, a trip lost, or an audit failed. FleetOS fixes each one where the work happens.

Problem

Paper manifests get lost

Trip sheets, signatures, and odometer readings on paper — lost, illegible, or never filed.

How FleetOS fixes it

Every trip is a digital record: GPS, timestamps, odometer, and both signatures captured in the driver’s browser and stored with the trip.

Problem

Medicaid claims get denied

A missing signature or timestamp is all it takes for a broker to deny a trip you already drove.

How FleetOS fixes it

A trip can’t be completed without its proof bundle. Dispatch sees “Ready to Bill” — or exactly what evidence is missing — before anything is submitted.

Problem

Expired credentials slip through

A CPR card or DOT medical lapses, a driver still gets dispatched, and the trip is unbillable — or worse.

How FleetOS fixes it

Credential expiry is tracked per driver with 30-day warnings, and dispatch is blocked from assigning a driver whose credentials have expired.

How it works

From application to billable trip in four steps

Driver and dispatch share one live trip record — every step below is visible on both sides the moment it happens.

01

Apply & get verified

Driver applies and uploads documents. Credentials are checked before any trip is assigned.

→
02

Dispatch assigns the trip

The trip appears on the driver’s manifest instantly — pickup, drop-off, rider needs, time.

→
03

Driver completes it in the browser

En Route, Arrived, Boarded, Complete — one tap per step, GPS-stamped, with securement and signatures.

→
04

Proof bundle is ready to bill

GPS, timestamps, odometer, and both signatures attach to the trip. Dispatch sees “Ready to Bill”.

Live sync, both ways. A driver tap updates dispatch instantly. A dispatch change re-orders the driver’s manifest. Offline taps queue and sync when the connection returns.

About FleetOS

Built for the fleets Medicaid depends on

FleetOS is the dispatch and audit operating system for non-emergency medical transportation providers. It was built for 5–25 vehicle fleets that live or die on clean trip records — dispatchers who need a live board, drivers who need a simple browser app, and owners who need every trip to survive a Medicaid audit.

No hardware to install, no app store to manage. Dispatch runs in the browser, drivers open their runs in the browser, and the proof for every trip is captured where the work happens.
